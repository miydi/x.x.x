# x.x.x | EEG-Based Personalized Music Therapy
I designed this project because I realized that most "mental health" apps actually add more stress by constantly asking us to report our feelings or fill out surveys. I wanted to create a system that works silently in the background—one that understands you without you having to say a single word.

# The Vision
The core idea behind x.x.x is to bridge the gap between high-tech neuroscience and daily life. I studied how EEG patterns correlate with stress and focus, but I don't want users to wear a headset all day. Instead, I'm using that data to build a smart engine that can adapt to you just by looking at how you interact with the app.

# What makes it different?
Passive Interaction: The system analyzes subtle signals like your color choices and task engagement patterns to adjust the therapy. No intrusive questions.
Layered Experience: I didn't just stop at "playing music." I designed a system architecture that includes "Silent Therapy" for sensory relief and a "Sanctuary Mode" for those moments when you just need to disconnect.
Privacy by Design: I made sure the logic focuses on behavioral patterns, not personal content, keeping the user’s privacy at the center.
